Folio by Approach

Get started

Install Folio, sign in, and join your class. It takes about 5 minutes.

Open Folio
  1. 1

    Install Folio on your phone

    Open the Folio link (or scan the QR code from your teacher).

    iPhone — use Safari

    Share → Add to Home Screen → Add

    Android — use Chrome

    Menu → Install app → Install

  2. 2

    Open Folio from your Home Screen

    Look for this icon. Always open Folio here, not in the browser.

  3. 3

    Sign in with Google

    Use your @approachisc.edu school account. A personal Gmail will not work.

  4. 4

    Check your name

    Write the name your teacher should call you.

  5. 5

    Join your class

    Tap Join a class and type the 6-letter code from your teacher, or scan the QR code.

    K7M2QX

  6. 6

    Turn on notifications

    When your phone asks, tap Allow. Folio tells you when a new week opens, when homework is due, and when your work is returned.

    iPhone: this needs iOS 16.4 or newer, and Folio opened from the Home Screen. You also get emails.

Need help?

  • “Please use your school account” — sign in again and choose your @approachisc.edu account.
  • The page says “Open in Safari or Chrome” — you opened the link inside another app. Copy the link and open it in your browser.
  • The code doesn’t work — check each letter, and ask your teacher for the code again.

Still stuck? Ask your teacher.
